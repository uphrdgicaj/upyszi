How does treatment work?

An IV treatment for depression is a simple process that takes less than an hour. One of our registered nurses comes to your home to administer the infusion. They will monitor your progress to ensure treatment is both safe and comfortable. Your nurse will also answer any questions you may have.

Many people begin to feel relief from symptoms within hours of the infusion. Ongoing treatments are most effective when used alongside counseling and coaching.

Patient Before and After-Care

Prep for an appointment:

Don’t eat anything 4-6 hours before treatment. Clear out your schedule for much of the day to lower your stress levels. It is not recommended to have the session late in the day as treatment may impact sleep.

Post-session:

Avoid large social gatherings and work obligations in the coming days as you may feel vulnerable. Avoid driving or the use of machinery for 4-6 hours after treatment.

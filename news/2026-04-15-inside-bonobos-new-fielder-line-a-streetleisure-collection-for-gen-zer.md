# Inside Bonobos’ New Fielder Line, a ‘Streetleisure’ Collection for Gen Zers

Our product picks are editor-tested, expert-approved. We may earn a commission through links on our site. Why Trust Us?

Bonobos has come a long way since first launching exclusively online in 2007. In just 15 years, the menswear brand opened several brick-and-mortar locations throughout the country, entered a partnership with Nordstrom, and then was acquired by Walmart. That’s quite a mouthful for an e-commerce company whose founders simply set out to create customizable pants that fit.

[Gen Zers](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/blog/9954246652.html)

And now Bonobos takes another big step with Fielder, a brand offshoot targeting clothes to Gen Zers. That's why the company is creating a line of "streetleisure"—the sweet spot where athleisure meets casual wear that’s fit for any occasion.

Also new is the Creator Collaborations, which sees Fielder teaming up with artists on designs every season. New York-based illustrator and animator Mike Perry—whose vibrant, fun flora-fueled work can be seen on the debut line’s spring/summer 2022 collection—is the first of the creative partnerships.

So, what exactly is this new Bonobos extension adding to its batch of menswear staples? More comfort and style, for sure. And plenty of versatility—whether it’s to work out, stay home in, or wear while discovering new places. It’s whatever you want it to be, a line of clothing that very much feels like the current vibe.

See for yourself by browsing some of our favorite tops, bottoms and outerwear from Fielder below, or head to Bonobos and Walmart, where you can shop the 2022 line in sizes ranging from XS–XXXL and with prices varying from $14 to $40.

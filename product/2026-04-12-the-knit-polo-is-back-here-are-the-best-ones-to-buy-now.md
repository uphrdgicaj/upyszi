What to Consider

Fabric & Knit Type

I recommend breathable, lightweight knits like cotton, linen blends, or viscose for warm weather, and wool or heavier cotton for cooler temps. Soft and breathable, perfect for everyday wear. Look for mercerized cotton if you want a subtle sheen and smoother texture.

Fit

A good knitted polo should skim the body without clinging. Slim or tailored fits are ideal for layering or wearing tucked in, while relaxed fits feel more retro and casual.

Collar Structure

Unlike traditional polos, knitted versions can have softer collars that lay flat or open more easily. Decide whether you prefer a structured button-up style or a more laid-back Johnny collar with no buttons.

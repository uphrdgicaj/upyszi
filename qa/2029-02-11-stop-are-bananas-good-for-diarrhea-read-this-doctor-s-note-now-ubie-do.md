Internal Medicine

Green bananas may help manage diarrhea due to their high content of resistant starch and pectin, a soluble fiber. These compounds absorb excess water in the intestines, help firm up loose stools, and support healthy gut bacteria. Green bananas are also a key part of the BRAT diet (bananas, rice, applesauce, toast), commonly recommended for digestive upset.

However, diarrhea can stem from many causes—ranging from mild dietary issues to infections or underlying conditions—so home remedies aren't always enough.

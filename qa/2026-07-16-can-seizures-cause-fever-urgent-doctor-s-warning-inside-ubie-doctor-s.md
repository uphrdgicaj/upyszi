# Can Seizures Cause Fever? Urgent Doctor’s Warning Inside | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Is it possible for seizures to cause a fever?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

[Doctor Warning Inside Ubie](https://dermaloom.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Internal Medicine

Seizures can sometimes cause a fever, though this is uncommon. A post-seizure fever, known as postictal hyperthermia, is most often associated with prolonged or complex partial seizures. It typically results from intense muscle activity, autonomic nervous system changes, or underlying inflammation triggered by the seizure itself.

However, fever and seizures can also occur in the reverse order—a fever may trigger a seizure, particularly in young children (febrile seizures). Because the relationship between fever and seizures can signal different underlying conditions, it's important to understand what may be causing your symptoms.

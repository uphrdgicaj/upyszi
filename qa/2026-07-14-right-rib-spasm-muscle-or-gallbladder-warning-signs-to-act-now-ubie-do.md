How do I know if a spasm under my right ribs is muscular or something more serious like gallbladder or liver issues?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Pain under the right ribs is often caused by painful rib syndrome, a musculoskeletal condition that produces sharp or aching discomfort along the lower rib cage. Unlike gallbladder or liver conditions, this pain typically stems from irritated intercostal nerves, strained muscles, or slipped rib cartilage. Other possible causes include muscle strain, rib injury, or referred pain from nearby organs.

Painful rib syndrome, also known as Cyriax syndrome, is a condition that can cause pain under the ribs. This pain is often sharp or aching and is caused by issues with the muscles or tissues around the ribs. It is different from problems with the gallbladder or liver, which can also cause pain in the same area.

Signs of Painful Rib Syndrome:

Location: Pain is often felt at the front of the chest or around the ribs.

Type of Pain: It is usually sharp or aching.

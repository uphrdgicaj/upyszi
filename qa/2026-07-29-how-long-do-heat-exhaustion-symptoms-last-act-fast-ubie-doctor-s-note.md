Explanation

Heat exhaustion happens when your body overheats and has too much stress from hot conditions. When you get out of the heat, start cooling down, and drink plenty of fluids, most of the uncomfortable symptoms like heavy sweating, weakness, and headache often start to get better within a few hours. However, how long these symptoms last can depend on how severe your condition is, how quickly you begin treatment, and whether you have any other health issues.

If you take action quickly by finding a cool place, resting, and hydrating, you might feel better fast. On the other hand, if the heat exposure was severe or if treatment takes a little longer, the symptoms could last as long as a day or two.

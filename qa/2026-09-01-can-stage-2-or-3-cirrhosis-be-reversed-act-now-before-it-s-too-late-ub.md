Can cirrhosis be stopped or reversed in stage 2 or 3?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Cirrhosis at stage 2 (compensated with fibrosis) or stage 3 (early decompensation) may be slowed, stabilized, or partially improved when the underlying cause—such as alcohol use, hepatitis B or C, or fatty liver disease—is treated early and consistently. However, full reversal of cirrhosis is rare because scar tissue in the liver is typically permanent. Outcomes depend on how much healthy liver tissue remains, how quickly treatment begins, and whether lifestyle changes are maintained.

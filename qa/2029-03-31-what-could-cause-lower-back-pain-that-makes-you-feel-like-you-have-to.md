Lower back pain that feels like the urge to poop is often caused by shared nerve pathways between the lower spine and digestive tract. Common causes include lumbar spine issues compressing nerves that signal the bowels, irritable bowel syndrome (IBS), constipation, endometriosis, or pelvic floor dysfunction. In some cases, this sensation can also point to more serious conditions like a herniated disc or cauda equina syndrome, which requires urgent care.

Lower back pain that makes you feel like you have to poop can be linked to nerve issues in the lower spine. The lumbosacral area of the spine can affect how we feel pain during activities like going to the bathroom. When nerves in this area are irritated or compressed, it can lead to pain that feels like it's connected to bowel movements.

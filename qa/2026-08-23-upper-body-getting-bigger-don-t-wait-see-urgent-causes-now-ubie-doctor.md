There are a few reasons why your upper body might be getting bigger.

Exercise and Muscle Growth:

When you do exercises like weightlifting, your muscles can grow bigger. This is called muscle hypertrophy. Your muscles get tiny tears when you lift weights, and when they heal, they grow bigger and stronger.

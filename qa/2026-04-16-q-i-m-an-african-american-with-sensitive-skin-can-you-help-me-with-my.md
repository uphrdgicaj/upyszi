Dear Jacques,

[Skin Can You Help](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Many people with deeper skin tones struggle with balancing blemished and sensitive skin. The key is to be consistent and gentle in your daily routine especially since an overly aggressive regimen can sensitize, increase oil production and hyper-pigment the skin. Wash and hydrate your face twice a day. Gently exfoliate two to three times a week and “spot treat” pimples as soon as you see or feel them creeping up. I like the Sircuit Skin Savior Problem Skin Cleanser. It has powerful botanicals (like tea tree, eucalyptus, clary, and lavender) that are naturally antibacterial, antimicrobial, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory. It also contains a lactic/salicylic acid blend that will gently eat away congestion in the skin. “Spot treating” is the best immediate way to treat pimples. Use either a clay based mask (like Dermalogica's Sebum Clearing Masque) or a salicylic acid based serum (like Sircuit's Fixzit Serum) on the affected area ONLY.

[Me with My Skin](https://benpepsi.com/blog/6713676236.html)

Shaving can be a tricky act for the skin, and razor bumps are annoying. But they should NOT be treated as acne. Exfoliate gently before shaving. This will not only lift the hair follicle for a closer shave, but will rid the skin of dead cells surrounding the pore. Moisturizing is an essential part of your daily routine, but it's especially important after shaving to protect the freshly exposed skin and to soothe the area. I like gel based moisturizers for men.

Lastly, let's address dry skin on the body. Exfoliate in the shower two to three times a week. You can use any body wash with a scrubbing brush or loofah. But for a super smooth finish, try a sugar scrub like Origin's Ginger Incredible Spreadable Scrub. Moisturize with a body cream or lotion as soon as you dry off. If you hate lotion, try aloe gel instead. For stubborn dry hands and feet my father (who constantly washes his hands as a nurse) has found that the pharmacist-developed Udderly Smooth products work miracles.

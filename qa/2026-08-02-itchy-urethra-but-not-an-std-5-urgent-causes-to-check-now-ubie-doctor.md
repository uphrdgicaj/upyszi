Explanation

Sometimes, when a man feels itchiness in his urethra and tests rule out classic sexually transmitted diseases (STDs), the cause can be another type of infection or irritation. One possibility is non-gonococcal urethritis, where bacteria like Mycoplasma or Ureaplasma, which are not classic STDs, may cause inflammation and itchiness. These germs are common and can sometimes lead to irritation even without the typical sexual transmission.

Other causes include chemical irritants from soaps, lotions, spermicides, or even laundry detergents that the area is exposed to. In addition, mechanical factors like irritation from too much friction during physical activities or minor injuries can also lead to an itchy feeling. It is important to note that while these causes are not the typical STDs, persistent itching or discomfort should be evaluated by a doctor to rule out any underlying issues or infections.

If you're experiencing these symptoms and want to better understand what might be causing them, you can check your symptoms to help identify potential causes and determine if you should seek medical attention. This helps ensure the right care is given and any necessary tests or treatments can be offered.

Explanation

To assess the risk of sleep apnea, quizzes typically include questions about common symptoms and risk factors. These may cover whether a person snores loudly, feels very sleepy during the day, or has been observed to stop breathing during sleep.

Common Questions in Sleep Apnea Quizzes:

Snoring: Do you snore loudly?

Daytime Sleepiness: Do you often feel tired or sleepy during the day?

Breathing Interruptions: Has anyone noticed you stop breathing during your sleep?

# Is Pain Relief Guaranteed After Weight Loss Surgery? Read This Now! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Are pain improvements guaranteed after weight loss surgery?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Weight loss surgery does not guarantee pain relief. While many patients experience reduced joint and back pain after significant weight loss, others continue to struggle with chronic pain post-surgery. This is especially true for individuals with underlying conditions such as lipedema, a fat disorder that does not respond to traditional weight loss methods and often causes persistent pain, swelling, and tenderness in the limbs.

If you're experiencing ongoing pain after weight loss surgery—or wondering whether a condition like lipedema could be the cause—it's important to identify the root issue before pursuing further treatment.

Is there a link between flavanols and mood improvement?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Cocoa flavanols, found in chocolate, may support better mood and sharper thinking. Research suggests these plant compounds can boost blood flow to the brain, enhance cognitive performance, and elevate mood by influencing neurotransmitters like serotonin and endorphins. Dark chocolate contains the highest concentration of flavanols, making it a potentially brain-friendly choice in moderation.

However, mood changes and brain fog can stem from many underlying causes—nutritional deficiencies, hormonal shifts, stress, or medical conditions—that chocolate alone won't fix. If you're experiencing persistent low mood, fatigue, or difficulty concentrating, it's worth understanding the root cause before self-treating.

# John Mayer Is Selling an Unauthorized Signature Nike Sneaker. Would You Wear Them?

Last week, John Mayer (a.k.a. Katy Perry's no. 1 former lover) spoke to GQ Style where, among the number of topics he discussed, he revealed that he had unofficially collaborated with Nike to make his own signature sneaker. In order to do this, Mayer didn’t technically team up with the world-famous brand, but instead created his own shoe on NikeID before buying every pair that the folks at Nike would sell to him.

“I think NikeiD is a little bit like iTunes University, where there’s this great resource that no one’s thinking to sort of hijack,” he said during the interview. “I’m planning on hijacking NikeiD and really messing with that to where I’m gonna have my own Nike shoe that Nike doesn’t have to give me.”

From there the 39-year-old singer-songwriter revealed what he plans to do with so many of the same pair of shoes. “I’m just waiting for it to get discontinued on their site,” he said. “And I’m gonna offer it as my own shoe through my Shopify page. Just for fun!"

The footwear that John opted to tinker with happens to be the Nike Air Max 90, which attained peak popularity a little over a decade ago. Mayer, a noted celebrity sneakerhead, customized a black, white, neon green, and grey bubble style, which he dubbed the “Spirit Level” Air Mayers.

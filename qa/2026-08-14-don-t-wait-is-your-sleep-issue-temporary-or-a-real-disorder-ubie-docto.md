How can I tell if my sleep problem is temporary or a true sleep disorder?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sleep problems are common and often temporary, typically caused by stress, travel, or changes in daily routine. However, when poor sleep lasts for weeks, disrupts your energy, mood, or ability to function during the day, it may signal a true sleep disorder such as insomnia, sleep apnea, or restless legs syndrome. Recognizing the difference between short-term sleep disruption and a chronic condition is the first step toward getting the right care.

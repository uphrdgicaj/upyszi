# Upgrade Your Underwear Drawer

Ask yourself this: When was the last time you freshened up your underwear drawer? We mean really sifted through it, tossing the clunkers, folding the keepers, and replacing the pairs you've had since college. It's a necessary excerise ever guy should commit to each season. But don't replace the ones that didn't make the cut with your same ol' go-tos. Do your research, and you're sure to get more mileage out of your next pair. Here, The Underwear Expert weighs in.

Triple up

Top men’s underwear brands are embracing three packs like never before, which means you get quality underwear for a bargain. Brands like Calvin Klein Underwear and Emporio Armani are joining fashion retailers like H&M and Uniqlo in offering some of their best designs for a fraction of the price. And these aren’t just black and white cotton skivvies either; your favorite brands are incorporating fashion colors and luxury fabric blends into the price busters which means you get awesome underwear x 3 for a fraction of the cost. That’s an equation we’re sure you can get behind.

Read reviews

There are tons of underwear reviews out there that will give you the good, the bad and the ugly on today’s hottest underwear trends. Both bloggers’ and consumers’ reviews are an easy way to get the unbiased truth before you make the buy. Underwear experience doesn’t really change all that much from person to person (at least in fabric quality, construction, cut, and so on) so the reviews will probably hold true for you, too. If they single out loose threads, uncomfortable pouches, or droopy backsides, stay away. For reviews you can trust, visit The Underwear Expert, or check out The Ultimate Guide to Underwear.

Choose long-lasting fabrics

Have you been putting off updating your drawer because it’s a hassle and hey, why not wear a pair until it falls apart? Tons of brands make super enduring fabric, which means they keep their shape, and take about 20 years to wear down. Brands like Pull-In and Freegun utilize a sublimated lycra and polyester blend in their underwear. You can stretch and pull ‘em for days and they’ll still maintain their shape—the underwear literally takes years, if not decades, to fade and come apart. A lot of guys are catching on to the hyper-cooling, ultra-wicking attributes found in athletic underwear, too, and are wearing them on a daily basis. C-in2’s super sturdy and fast-drying Grip underwear is a prime example of athletic underwear that goes the distance.

Looking up a brand’s sizing chart before you buy means a perfect fit and less money down the drain. We all know you can’t return underwear, so this is important (and easy). There’s no standard sizing for men’s underwear, so a medium that works for you in Calvin Klein, won’t necessarily work in other brands. Some of finding the right pair is trial and error, too, because not all sizing is true to the fit, but sizing charts a great place to start to get your drawer updated with underwear that fits like a glove.

And remember: No matter what you choose, be sure to wash them properly. High heat is an instant underwear killer. It fades the color and decreases fabric’s ability to maintain shape—even the sturdiest cottons can’t keep it together. A general rule of thumb is just to follow the washing instructions on the tag; most underwear requires cold water, no bleach and either an air or tumble dry on low heat. That’s not so bad is it? Give it a try—your new underwear will last 10 times longer.

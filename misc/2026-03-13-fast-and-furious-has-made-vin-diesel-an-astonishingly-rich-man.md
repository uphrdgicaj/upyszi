Here's something you might not know about Vin Diesel: Before his career exploded with the Fast and Furious franchise, the California native was an indie filmmaker.

Vin Diesel — real name Mark Sinclair — wasn't about to wait around to become famous. In the early '90s, he dropped out of college, left his job as a bouncer, and started work on his very first film, Multi-Facial. The multi-talented Diesel wrote, produced, directed, and starred in this short film, which made its way to the Cannes Film Festival.

He then went on to direct, write, and star in his first feature, Strays, which also made it to Sundance. Though neither film made him any money, they both made an impact: According to Biography, Steven Spielberg happened to see Diesel's Strays, and wrote a part specifically for him in Saving Private Ryan. That role earned him his first $100,000 pay day.

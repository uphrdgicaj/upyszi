On this current season of Survivor: Edge of Extinction, Keith Sowell and Chris Underwood had a feud that seemed to stem from when Chris helped to vote out Keith in Episode 2. Their rivalry only continued when they both landed on Extinction Island and Chris literally pulled an advantage out of Keith's hands as they struggled for it in the sand.

However, Keith is now clearing up why his dislike for Chris is so strong. In an interview with Entertainment Weekly, the 19-year-old explained that Chris insulted something that is "off-limits" to him.

"After he got the advantage, everything went downhill. When I’m stressed to that extent, I pray because that’s my safe space. At that point, Chris says, 'Oh, you prayin’ now?' and continued to, what I believed, insult my faith which is off-limits for me," Keith explained. "A culmination of things led to a breakdown that I take joy in overcoming, and developed this whole David and Goliath dynamic."

Keith's faith is very important to him, and he was seen praying many times during the season, most notably when he was given the chance to go to Extinction after being voted out.

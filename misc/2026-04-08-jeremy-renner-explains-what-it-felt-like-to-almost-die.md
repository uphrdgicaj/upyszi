# Jeremy Renner Explains What It Felt Like to Almost Die

[Like to](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/0282211018.html)

[Jeremy Renner Explains What](https://glp-insights.xianfengchem.com/blog/7048544511.html)

IN A NEW cover story for Men's Health, Jeremy Renner recounts his near-fatal accident in January of 2023. The actor saved his nephew from being run over by a snowplow, but in the act of bravery he was run over himself. Renner succumbed to 38+ injuries ranging from a broken pelvis to his eyeball popping out of his socket. When he spoke to MH in detail about those moments under the snowplow, he reflects on what exactly he experienced during his brush with death.

[What It Felt](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/blog/7158873398.html)

"There’s no time, place, or space," he recalls. "It’s magnificent. It’s the mind’s eye. Not your vision. You don’t need vision—you’re dead. Vision is part of the stupid body thing. But the mind’s eye you take with you. You see in your mind—that’s being dead. That’s what it’s like being dead. What you can visualize with your eyes closed. It’s awesome. It’s awesome! And by the way, everyone’s in it! It’s what your imagination is. It was joy. It was exhilarating peace. Exhilarating peace. You’re connected to everything all at once. All the love from the third-grade teacher you fell in love with to the everything all at once. All that’s there. Continual. Perpetual. In perpetuity. It’s infinite. It’s magnificent."

Despite how peaceful that may have felt, Renner is, of course, glad he's alive. As he moves forward from the accident, now changed forever both mentally and physically, he vows to never have a bad day again. "I’m glad I’m here, and I’m going to keep feeding what you take with you: those shared experiences with those you love," he says. "It’s eternal, and you take it with you. It’s connected."

# The Rock Cast John Cena, His Former WWE Rival, in a New Movie

The Rock and John Cena's heartwarming journey from bitter in-ring rivals to Hollywood's buffest buds just hit a new milestone.

[John Cena](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Dwayne Johnson has cast fellow WWE star John Cena in the The Janson Directive, co-produced by Universal and The Rock's Seven Buck Productions.

The Rock announced the casting on Instagram, promising a producer-style People's Elbow for Cena if the star gets out of line. "Found my star!" he wrote in a post miraculously not set in a gym at 3 in the morning.

"Congrats man and I’m excited to go on this journey with you," Johnson wrote. "Thank you for your commitment to the role and remember, as your producer if you screw up, I’m coming to set and droppin’ the People’s Elbow on ya. And you know first hand how devastating my elbow drops can be."

[John Cena](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/)

The Rock and John Cena

Johnson and Cena do in fact have a storied past together, performing across the ring in two consecutive WrestleMania main events (including one, as he mentions in the post, in which The Rock ripped a few muscles away from his pelvis, as one does). The pair are best buds now — although apparently with very differing opinions on having children — and The Janson Directive marks the next step in Cena's very Rock-like path from the ring to the silver screen.

According to The Hollywood Reporter, the film — based on a bestselling novel by Jason Bourne creator Robert Ludlum — follows "a former Navy SEAL who now works as a corporate security consultant" who "becomes a target himself when a rescue operation goes horribly wrong."

Throat swelling can be uncomfortable and may be caused by various conditions. There are several methods to help reduce this swelling:

Flurbiprofen Lozenges: According to research, flurbiprofen lozenges, which contain 8.75 mg of the active ingredient, can effectively reduce throat swelling by decreasing inflammation. These lozenges are often used for their soothing effects on the throat.

Nimesulide: This medication is known for treating acute inflammation in the upper respiratory tract. It works by reducing the substances in the body that cause inflammation and swelling.

Dexamethasone: Used particularly in patients with head and neck issues, dexamethasone is a type of steroid that helps in reducing inflammation and swelling. It is often prescribed in more severe cases where other treatments might not be sufficient.

These medications work by targeting inflammation, which is a common cause of swelling.

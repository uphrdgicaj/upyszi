# Under Eye Injection Filler Training using Stylage S

Preview

About this lesson

Refine your approach to under-eye rejuvenation with Hubmed Ed’s Level B training focused on volume restoration and precision.

In this video course, Dr Juan Sopena begins by assessing the tear trough deformity, noting subtle volume loss and areas requiring soft, controlled correction. He demonstrates how to select Stylage filler for its suitability in the delicate under-eye region, followed by precise aliquot placement to restore smoothness. Throughout the treatment, he evaluates symmetry, adjusts technique according to depth, and ensures natural, refreshed results with minimal product.

‍

Things you’ll learn

• How to assess tear trough deformities and identify key areas requiring subtle correction

• How to select the appropriate Stylage product for safe, effective under-eye rejuvenation

• How to apply small, controlled aliquots to achieve smooth, natural-looking volume restoration

• How to evaluate treatment progress from multiple angles and refine results in real time

Why Take this Course?

This focused tear trough training is ideal for intermediate injectors looking to achieve safe and effective under-eye correction. You’ll improve your understanding of volume loss patterns, product behaviour in thin tissue, and how to deliver natural results that reduce tiredness without overcorrection. It also strengthens your confidence in using controlled aliquots to achieve optimal precision.

Learning Outcomes:

• Assess tear trough deformity and volume loss to guide your treatment decisions

• Select and use Stylage S effectively for delicate under-eye rejuvenation

• Apply controlled aliquots with precision for smooth, natural correction

Why We Chose This

Most B vitamins don't stick around in your body, so consistent dietary intake matters. The catch: converting standard B vitamins into forms your cells can actually use requires a functioning liver and healthy enzymes — something that doesn't always happen smoothly with age, certain medications, or genetic variations. Basic B Complex sidesteps this problem by providing pre-activated B vitamins, eliminating the conversion step entirely. This matters especially for vegetarians, vegans, and older adults, who often face absorption challenges. The yeast-free formulation also removes a common allergen found in many B-complex supplements.

It may reduce acne and excess oil, calm redness and rosacea, fade dark spots, shrink enlarged pores, and improve fine lines for clearer, smoother, more even-looking skin.

SOME CALL them trousers, some call them slacks, while others may refer to them as suit pants. Regardless of what you call dress pants for men, they are absolute wardrobe essentials these days. Athleisure may have ruled for a few years but the time has come to trade in some of your sweatpants for a more elevated look. Whether you're back in the office or not, you should keep a few pairs of dress pants in steady rotation. There is no better way to step up your date night look or impress at casual get-togethers. However, that doesn’t mean that you have to button up with a blazer and tie every time you dawn slacks. Dress pants for men work perfectly well with t-shirts or crewneck sweatshirts and loafers for a high-low look. Once winter starts to roll in, some pleated flannel slacks match perfectly with your coziest wool sweaters. Or take the middle ground with an oxford shirt, tucked or untucked, it works either way.

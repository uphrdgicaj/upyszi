Explanation

After giving birth, a woman's body goes through many changes that can affect her menstrual cycle. For women with PCOS, these changes can lead to irregular periods.

Hormonal Changes:

According to research, the body undergoes hormonal shifts after childbirth. These changes can affect the regularity of the menstrual cycle, making periods irregular.

Breastfeeding Effects:

Breastfeeding can also impact ovulation and fertility. When a woman is breastfeeding, her body produces hormones that can delay the return of regular menstrual cycles. This is because breastfeeding naturally suppresses ovulation.

These factors combined can contribute to why a woman with PCOS might experience irregular periods after having a baby. It is important to remember that each woman's experience can be different, and the return to regular cycles may vary.

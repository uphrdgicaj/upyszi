# Post Treatment Care

Post Treatment Care

Lash Lift and Tint Post-Treatment Instructions

[Post Treatment Care](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/)

Immediately Post

[Post Treatment Care](https://appetitecode.jcesqc8.com/)

Lashes are very fragile and still adjusting to the lift solutions and shape! If any tinting color remains around the eye area, gently use a wet cotton swab to remove, avoiding eyelash hair.

24 Hours Post

Keep eye area clean and dry (do not soak or cleanse)

Avoid rubbing the eye area and sleeping on lashes

Avoid mascara and eye makeup

Day 1

This is the most important period of the eyelash lift aftercare. For the first 24 hours, you need to be extra careful and follow all the tips listed above: don’t get your lashes wet, and don’t apply any products onto them.

You might experience very slight redness and irritation. Apply a cold compress if it gets uncomfortable.

[Post Treatment Care](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/blog/8451492147.html)

If your lashes were tinted, stay away from sunlight.

[Post Treatment Care](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Day 2

[Post Treatment Care](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/)

[Post Treatment Care](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/blog/7082269849.html)

If the redness and irritation hasn’t subsided, contact your technician.

Your lashes are still flexible, so you still need to be careful and avoid water, especially hot, steam, rubbing your eyes and sleeping on your face. Since they’re so elastic, you don’t want them to change shape!

Wait a little bit longer before applying eye makeup.

Day 3

You can relax now and start behaving as usual.

However, it’s advisable to still avoid mascara, especially waterproof formulas. They might contain oily ingredients which can relax the curl. Do not rub your eyes and avoid using aggressive makeup removers – especially oil-based ones.

Days 4-20

You don’t have to do anything special anymore, and you can go back to all your normal activities, just keep avoiding oil-based products as well as those that contain exfoliates. They can relax the curl.

Day 20+

If your treatment included tinting, the color will start to wear off at this point.

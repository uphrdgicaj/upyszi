# These 16 Kitchens Will Make You Want to Cook

If you have a dingy, depressing kitchen, you probably don't want to spend that much time there—which isn't doing your waistline any favors. New research shows the key to weight loss might be eating at home, so now is a good time to start thinking about that kitchen renovation.

To get you started, here are 16 Instagrams of kitchens awesome enough to make you put down the takeout menu.

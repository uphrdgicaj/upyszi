What is Pyoderma Gangrenosum?

It is a non-inflammatory, non-infectious skin condition that causes large, painful skin ulcers to develop. These begin as small red bumps or blisters. The cause is unknown, but it is associated with immune system disorders like rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, and ulcerative colitis.

Typical Symptoms of Pyoderma Gangrenosum

Pus-filled blisters or bumps on the skin

Scars or marks from the rash/wound

Diagnosed with Crohn's disease in the past

Hard or stiff skin

Areas of irritated or peeling skin

Previously diagnosed with Ulcerative Colitis

History of connective tissue, autoimmune or rheumatic disease

Blisters on the skin

Treatment of Pyoderma Gangrenosum

Treatment involves administering anti-inflammatory medication and proper wound management to prevent infection. Skin grafting may be required for large wounds.

# Watch These Calisthenics Athletes Crush the U.S. Army Fitness Test

The U.S. Army Physical Fitness Test is a popular challenge among the YouTube community, with bodybuilders and professional athletes alike struggling to meet the functional fitness requirements for basic training. In his latest video, calisthenics vloggers Stan Browney and Arjen Albers takes on the test—consisting of 2 minutes of pushups, 2 minutes of situps, and a 2-mile run—with no prior training.

They start with the pushup test, which requires a minimum of 42 reps to pass (a "perfect" score is 71 or above). The reps must be performed with the arms at a 90-degree angle to the ground, shoulder-width apart, and if either of them wants to take a rest at any time, they are only able to do so in a downward dog position.

Albers does his pushups first, and manages 82, a better-than-perfect figure. Browney goes next and narrowly beats Albers, completing 83 reps in the allotted 2 minutes. "First 100 points are in!" He says, before heading straight into round two: the situps.

In the PFT, 53 situp reps are required to pass, while 78 or above constitutes a perfect score. "That was really hard," says Albers after he performs 67 reps in 2 minutes. "I feel it in my quads as well; at one point your abs give out, and your quads take over." This time, Browny falls one behind Albers, managing just 66 situps. "I thought the running was going to be the worst, but apparently, I hope, it's the situps," he says.

For the final round of the test, the 2-mile run, a passing time is anything under 15:54, while a perfect score would be 13 minutes or under. Albers just misses out on a perfect time at 13:13, while Browney completes his run in 13:48 before collapsing. "Everything is spinning," he says. "I literally gave it my all."

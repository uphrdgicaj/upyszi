Clinical Evidence of Benefit

Kim JH et al. (2008)

Design: Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial

Participants: 45 men with mild to moderate ED

Dosage: 900 mg or 1,200 mg Korean red ginseng, three times daily for 8 weeks

Significant improvement in International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF) scores versus placebo

Increased peak penile blood flow measured by Doppler ultrasound

Treatment was well tolerated, with only mild headaches or indigestion in a few participants

Gauthaman K et al. (2010)

Analysis: Review of clinical and animal studies on Panax ginseng

Consistent improvements in erection quality at doses of 600–1,000 mg, three times daily

Animal data show restoration of NO synthase activity in corpora cavernosa

Safe profile in most healthy adults, including those over 65

Together, these studies support Korean red ginseng for erectile dysfunction as a viable natural option for older men.

Potential Interactions & Precautions

While generally safe, Korean red ginseng can interact with certain medications and conditions:

• Anticoagulants (e.g., warfarin) – Ginseng may reduce warfarin's effectiveness, raising clot risk.

• Blood pressure drugs – Possible additive effects on hypertension or hypotension.

• Diabetes medications – Ginseng can lower blood sugar; monitor for hypoglycemia.

• Stimulant effects – Insomnia, nervousness, or jitteriness at high doses.

• Hormone-sensitive conditions – Limited data suggest mild estrogen-like activity; men with prostate cancer should consult a doctor.

Always review your current prescriptions and medical history with a healthcare provider before starting any new supplement.

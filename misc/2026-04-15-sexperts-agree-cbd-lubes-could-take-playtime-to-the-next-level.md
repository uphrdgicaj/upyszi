What to Look for in a CBD Lube?

Forgive the obviousness of this statement, but if you’re going to introduce something into or onto your body, you want to make sure you’re not allergic to it first.

Ingredients

Do yourself a favor and read the ingredient label and make sure you don’t have an intolerance to any of the ingredients listed. In particular, pay attention if you have a nut allergy as almond oil and coconut oil make a regular appearance in CBD-infused lubes and arousal oils.

CBD Amount

Once you’ve ruled out possible allergens, spend some time looking at how much CBD is in each bottle. Given the slack of robust research performed about sexuality, as well as on CBD, it should come as no surprise that at the time of publishing there hasn’t been any conclusive evidence on how much CBD needs to be in each bottle or pump to be effective.

In general, a good rule of thumb is to look for a product that contains at least 200 mg of broad spectrum CBD. Assuming the bottle is small, as most CBD lubricant packages are, this means about 2 mg of CBD will be in each pump. (Though, people who are sensitive to CBD may enjoy benefits from products with half that).

Unless you have a known issue with pain during penetration, start with a lower amount of CBD, suggests Reeves. Then switch to a product with a higher amount down the road if you find you want to. Or, you can simply add more pumps. “You can slowly increase the amount of CBD-infused lube or oil you use over time, experimenting until you find an amount that works well for you and your body, and re-apply as needed,” she says. (FYI: It typically takes about 20 to 30 minutes for the CBD in the lubricant to absorb to the area it was applied).

Wait, is CBD Lube Safe?

Yes! Generally speaking, CBD is considered incredibly safe, according to Reeves.

Actually, it's the lack of lubrication that is considered unsafe. Lubrication is essential for some degree of slide-and-glide that helps make penetration feel so good, she explains. Without it, an individual is at increased risk for an undue amount of friction that can increase the occurrence of micro tears. These micro tears make an individual more susceptible to all types of infections, as well as inflammation, hemorrhoids, and other anal and genital ouchies.

Still, before you start slathering your genitals in a new product, Reeves recommends doing a skin test. “ Each person and their experience with CBD is different, so it’s best to test on the inside of your arm or leg to make sure you aren’t sensitive to any of the ingredients,” she says.

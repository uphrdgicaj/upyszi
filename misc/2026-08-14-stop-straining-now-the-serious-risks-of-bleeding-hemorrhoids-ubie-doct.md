Can frequent straining to poop lead to complications like bleeding or hemorrhoids?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, frequently straining to poop can cause health problems, including rectal bleeding, hemorrhoids, anal fissures, and even pelvic floor dysfunction over time. Chronic straining puts pressure on the veins and tissues around the anus, which can lead to pain, discomfort, and lasting damage if left unaddressed.

Explanation

When you strain a lot during bowel movements, it means you are putting extra pressure on the blood vessels in and around your bottom. This pressure may cause the veins to swell and become irritated, leading to hemorrhoids, which are like soft bumps near your skin. Hemorrhoids can sometimes bleed when they are hurt or irritated, causing small amounts of blood to appear when you wipe or go to the bathroom.

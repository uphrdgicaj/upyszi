Safety

The rating we arrived at here for safety combines the well-established side effect profiles of these three drugs with a comparison of available doses in similar chewables from competitors.5

On the safety research side, PDE5 inhibitors have been known to cause various side effects, including muscle pain, decreased blood pressure, vision changes, and even nasal congestion.6 Many of these side effects arise from the collateral inhibition of phosphodiesterase enzymes other than PDE5. Research indicates that inhibition of PDE6, for example, can cause “functional blindness, blue (cynopsia) and blurred vision, and enhanced light sensitivity … upon intake of PDE5 inhibitors in patients with ED.”7

Specifically, PDE6 inhibition is more associated with sildenafil, while inhibition of PDE11, which can cause muscle pain and may affect sperm quality, is associated with tadalafil.8 9

Because these individual drugs have slightly different side effect profiles, it’s helpful that Hims offers them individually as well as in combination with one another, so you and your consulting physician can balance efficacy and safety.

What are Hims Hard Mints?

Hims Hard Mints are chewable ED medications compounded in mint-flavored tablets that look and taste like popular breath mints you might find at the checkout counter of any grocery store. They even have the little green speckling on the white tablets to visually indicate their mintiness, and they come in a slide-top tin you can carry around in your pocket much more comfortably than a prescription pill bottle.

The mints are designed to contain one or two out of three popular ED medications: sildenafil, tadalafil, and vardenafil. They’re available in prescriptions intended for as-needed use or, for certain ingredients, daily use.

Keep in mind that these mints are not designed to work sublingually; the drugs don’t pass through oral membranes to enter the bloodstream before normal metabolism.10 You eat them, they endure hepatic metabolism, and they take the same amount of time to work as if you’d taken them in normal pill form.11 That differentiates them from a competing product from Ro, called Ro Sparks, which are designed to dissolve under the tongue and potentially work a little faster. Hard Mints have a little more dose flexibility than Sparks, though, and their lower available doses make them a better choice for most men new to ED medications.

How do Hims Hard Mints work?

To understand how Hims Hard Mints work, we need to look at the way that each of the drugs available in Hard Mint form functions to improve erectile health. None of these drugs will just give you an erection; that’s not what things like Viagra are actually designed to do. Instead, they create the environment for you to achieve a healthy erection in response to stimuli.

All three belong to a specific drug class called PDE5 inhibitors, though there are some noteworthy differences among them.

As PDE5 inhibitors, they each reduce the expression of phosphodiesterase-5, an enzyme that helps your body stop having an erection after climax or the removal of sufficient stimuli.12 Without it, you would have an erection that never went away (not a great idea). But if your body’s timing is off, or blood flow is insufficient to outperform PDE5’s influence, you could experience ED.

By inhibiting PDE5, these drugs allow blood to flow freely when you’re stimulated, and they prevent an erection from going away before you’re done using it.13 At the right dose, they should be strong enough to satisfy your sexual needs but weak enough that your erection can go away naturally after intercourse.

The drugs in question are sildenafil (generic for Viagra), tadalafil (generic for Cialis), and vardenafil (generic for Levitra). Let’s take a closer look at each so you know what to discuss with a consulting physician about what might be best for you:

Sildenafil

Sildenafil is a reliable and relatively inexpensive choice for most men looking to treat their ED with a prescription medication. However, its side effect profile and duration of action (6-8 hours) may make it an inferior choice to tadalafil, which lasts much longer.14

Tadalafil

Tadalafil is also a PDE5 inhibitor, but it can be taken in two different ways and lasts longer in the bloodstream than either sildenafil or vardenafil. You can take it as needed about an hour before sex, and it will last for some time between 18 and 36 hours.15 You can also take a typically smaller dose on a daily basis to be ready for sex whenever the mood arises.

Vardenafil

Vardenafil is decidedly similar to sildenafil in both its duration of action and its efficacy. Despite being similar to sildenafil in these ways, some research indicates that vardenafil can be taken daily as a treatment for ED, especially in patients whose ED is rooted in complications from diabetes.16 Hims is one of the few providers we’ve seen to offer daily-dose vardenafil, and it does so at an exceptional price.

The most important consideration when it comes to the safety of Hims Hard Mints is that they’re only available by prescription. A Hims-affiliated physician or nurse practitioner will review your medical history to determine your eligibility for treatment.

That level of scrutiny helps protect you from dangerous contraindications and should safeguard you against the most severe potential side effects. Among these, cardiovascular risk factors are of high concern, and your consulting physician may require blood pressure readings or a self-reported fitness assessment before prescribing any PDE5 inhibitor.

However, PDE5 inhibitors have other specific, often less serious side effect risks that don’t all preclude potential patients from taking them. Your doctor can give you a prescription, but you might still have an adverse reaction to the medication.

For example, flushing and headaches are common with PDE5 inhibitors, with a higher percentage of sildenafil users reporting them than tadalafil users.18 Conversely, a higher percentage of tadalafil users report muscle pain than sildenafil users.19 This goes back to the collateral inhibition of other PDE enzymes that we discussed earlier, whereby a specific PDE5 inhibitor may unintentionally inhibit PDE1, 6, or 11, causing particular side effects.

Vardenafil is closer in its molecular structure to sildenafil than tadalafil, but it’s a newer medication than sildenafil, and it does a better job of exclusively targeting PDE5. Specifically, it seems to avoid PDE6, meaning that it doesn’t cause the color perception effects associated with sildenafil.20 That said, vardenafil can interfere with the timing of heartbeats, making it a nonstarter for men with things like prolonged QT syndrome.21

Ultimately, the picture of cardiac health and potential contraindications is complicated enough that these drugs will remain prescription-only for the foreseeable future. This ensures a baseline of safety for men with ED, and you should certainly bring up any concerns about heart health or other potential issues during your intake.

Alternatives to Hims Hard Mints

To make sense of your options for treating ED outside of Hims Hard Mints, we should divide the market between similar prescription providers and alternatives to prescription intervention. Let’s start with available prescription alternatives.

Prescription alternatives to Hims Hard Mints

Hims Hard Mints are not the only chewable ED medication option on the market, nor are they necessarily the best choice for you. Other companies offer different drug and dose combinations, and even different flavors and delivery methods (e.g., lozenges instead of chewables). Many of those companies, and others, also offer telemedical access to traditional ED pills, as well, which can often be less expensive than more novel chewables.

Here’s a quick look at providers and options for prescription treatment outside of Hims Hard Mints:

Ro Sparks

Ro Sparks contain sildenafil and tadalafil at a normal starting dose for the former and a higher dose than usual for the latter. Specifically, they contain 50mg of sildenafil and 22mg of tadalafil. That’s more than double the usual 10mg starting dose for as-needed tadalafil, and it’s what makes Sparks less than ideal for men who haven’t tried ED medications yet.

Still, Sparks are extremely popular, especially among men who feel they’re ready to try a slightly stronger medication. They come as individually wrapped sublingual lozenges designed for you to hold under your tongue and allow to dissolve. The process takes a while (at least 8-12 minutes, based on our testing), which may be uncomfortable for some men to maintain during foreplay.

BlueChew

BlueChew was the originator of chewable ED medications, but Hims and Ro left them behind for a brief time with novel drug combinations, higher doses, and better prices. It took some time for BlueChew to catch up, but now it offers things like chewable daily tadalafil and a combination of tadalafil with either sildenafil or vardenafil that rivals Ro Sparks.

Not only does BlueChew’s Max Combo have more flexibility than Ro Sparks, but it’s also significantly less expensive, costing as little as $4.58/dose. Meanwhile, Sparks have a set price of $12/dose, no matter how many you buy.

Rugiet

Rugiet’s original product, Rugiet Ready, was a sublingual lozenge like Ro Sparks, but it contained a combination of sildenafil, tadalafil, and apomorphine in one of four strengths you could customize further in consultation with a Rugiet physician. Apomorphine is an intriguing addition — it’s not a PDE5 inhibitor, but studies point toward its efficacy against ED nonetheless.22 The company has expanded its offerings to include a combination ED and premature ejaculation pill as well as a chewable tadalafil.

As was the case with Rudiet Ready, the company’s newer products are also more expensive than their competitors’ options.

Traditional ED pills

BlueChew is the only company we mention in this review that does not offer traditional ED pills (the kind you need to swallow with a glass of water). Hims, Ro, and Rugiet all have at least one pill-based ED treatment, with Hims offering outstanding prices on daily tadalafil and Strut Health offering the best savings on sildenafil.

Nonprescription ED treatments outside of Hims Hard Mints

If you’re looking for alternatives to Hims Hard Mints because you’d rather avoid the prescription route altogether, there are several options you can explore, some of which boast a bit more scientific credibility than others. Here, we explore those non-Rx options:

Mental health care

A research paper from 2021 reported that up to 40% of ED cases are psychogenic, meaning they’re rooted in some psychological issue like anxiety or depression.28 Talk therapy and cognitive behavioral therapy can potentially relieve various symptoms of anxiety and depression, and it may be an excellent place to start for men with ED who also have mental health concerns.29

It’s also not unusual for ED to contribute to anxiety and depression, creating a negative feedback loop that makes the problem continue to get worse.30 Treating it with talk therapy or medications for anxiety or depression may make a significant positive difference.

Penis rings

If penis pumps are all about drawing blood to the penis, penis rings are about keeping it there. Nutritional supplements

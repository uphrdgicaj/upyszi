Urgent care centers in the U.S. typically stay open beyond regular business hours, with most offering extended evening and weekend availability to serve patients when primary care offices are closed. Common hours include 8 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and shorter windows on Saturdays and Sundays, though hours vary by location and provider. Many urgent care clinics accept walk-ins without appointments, making them a convenient option for non-life-threatening conditions like minor injuries, infections, or sudden illness.

According to research, these centers typically operate during evenings and weekends, making them accessible when many primary care offices might be closed.

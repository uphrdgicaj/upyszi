Doctor's Note: GLP-1 Protein Goals for Black Women to Keep Their Curves

GLP-1 medications (like semaglutide or tirzepatide) are powerful tools for weight management. They curb appetite, help with blood sugar control and can promote fat loss. For Black women, who often carry more lean muscle mass and value their natural curves, preserving muscle while slimming down is key. Protein becomes your best ally—to maintain strength, shape and overall health.

Below, we break down evidence-based protein targets, practical tips and cultural considerations so you hit your "curve-saving" goals without guesswork.

Why Protein Matters on GLP-1s

[Ubie Doctor's Note](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Appetite Suppression: GLP-1s reduce hunger. That's great for fat loss, but it can make reaching protein targets harder.

Muscle Preservation: Higher protein intake helps protect and build lean mass. This keeps curves lifted and metabolism humming.

Satiety & Energy: Protein is filling. It stabilizes blood sugar and supports recovery from daily activities or workouts.

How Much Protein Do You Really Need?

Standard RDA (0.8 g per kg body weight) is a minimal amount to prevent deficiency. On GLP-1s, experts recommend higher intakes to preserve muscle:

1.2 – 1.6 g per kg of "ideal" body weight• For a 70 kg (154 lb) woman, that's 84 – 112 g protein per day.

0.5 – 0.7 g per lb of body weight• A 154 lb woman: 77 – 108 g protein per day.

20 – 30% of total calories from protein• On a 1,500 kcal plan → 75 – 113 g protein.

Aim near the upper end if you're very active, worried about muscle loss or have experienced rapid weight loss.

Calculating Your Personal Protein Goal

Determine your target weight or current weight (in kg or lb).

Multiply by 1.2 – 1.6 g/kg (or 0.5 – 0.7 g/lb).

Round to the nearest 5 g for simplicity.

Example for a 160 lb Black woman: • 160 lb × 0.6 g = 96 g protein/day • Distribute evenly across meals/snacks.

Timing & Distribution

Rather than loading at one meal, spread protein evenly:

[Women Need on GLP-](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/7580271333.html)

Breakfast: 25 – 30 g

Lunch: 25 – 30 g

Dinner: 25 – 30 g

Optional Snack: 10 – 15 g

Why? Muscle protein synthesis needs a "leucine threshold" (~2.5 g per meal). Even spacing optimizes muscle repair and fullness.

Practical Tips to Hit Your Targets

Prep Ahead: Grill chicken, boil eggs or cook lentils in bulk.

Protein-Packed Snacks: Beef jerky, protein bars, nuts + cheese.

Smoothie Hack: 1 scoop protein powder + ½ cup Greek yogurt + berries + spinach.

Smart Swaps:• Replace rice with quinoa.• Use beans in soups, salads or wraps.

Meal Recomposition: If appetite dips, reduce volume but keep protein portions intact.

Bone Health: Balance protein with calcium and vitamin D (from dairy or fortified plant milks).

Flavor Profiles: Season proteins with spices common in African, Caribbean or Southern cuisines (e.g., jerk seasoning, smoked paprika, garlic).

Monitoring & Adjusting

Track protein intake for 1–2 weeks using an app or food journal.

Notice energy levels, muscle soreness and any changes in measurements (waist, hips).

Adjust up or down by 5–10 g if you're under your goal or feeling overly full.

What Is Shilajit?

Shilajit is a sticky, tar-like substance found primarily in the Himalayan mountains. It forms over centuries, as plant matter decomposes and interacts with minerals trapped in rock. Key components include:

Fulvic AcidA potent antioxidant that helps transport nutrients into cells, supports detoxification, and combats oxidative stress.

Scientific Support for Healthy Aging

Multiple studies have explored how Shilajit may support longevity and healthy aging:

Mitochondrial FunctionResearch in the "Journal of Ethnopharmacology" shows fulvic acid can enhance mitochondrial ATP production, giving cells more energy and resilience.

Antioxidant CapacityA 2016 study found Shilajit supplementation reduced markers of oxidative stress in animal models, suggesting potential to counter age-related cellular damage.

Anti-Inflammatory ActionChronic, low-grade inflammation accelerates aging. Shilajit's bioactive compounds have been shown to lower pro-inflammatory cytokines in preclinical studies.

By combining these mechanisms, Shilajit may help you maintain physical performance, cognitive clarity, and overall wellness as you age.

While Shilajit is generally well tolerated, be aware of:

Contaminants: Low-quality products may contain heavy metals or mycotoxins.

Interactions: It may alter blood pressure or anticoagulant medications.

Allergic Reactions: Rare, but discontinue use if you experience rash, itching, or gastrointestinal upset.

Always inform your healthcare provider of any supplements you take to ensure comprehensive care.

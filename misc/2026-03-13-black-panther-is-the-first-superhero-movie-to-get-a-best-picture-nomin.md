# 'Black Panther' Is the First Superhero Movie to Get a Best Picture Nomination

Black Panther has once again made some firsts.

The mega-hit on Tuesday notched seven Academy Award nominations, including for Best Picture, making it not just the first Marvel offering to be nominated in the Oscars’ top category, but the first ever superhero movie to do so.

Comic book films have typically fared well at the box office, but are not well represented at the big awards shows. And if they are recognized at all during awards season, it’s usually for technical achievements.

But Black Panther transcends the superhero genre, pairing smart social commentary with a deeply human story, all of it anchored by strong performances by Chadwick Boseman, Michael B. Jordan, Letitia Wright, and the rest of the cast. Indeed, it wasn’t just a movie — it became something of a movement, with its iconic “Wakanda forever!” rallying cry taking on a life of its own.

The Ryan Coogler-directed blockbuster already made history with its Best Picture - Drama nomination at the Golden Globes; it lost out to the Freddie Mercury biopic, Bohemian Rhapsody, at the awards earlier this month.

It is up against BlacKkKlansman, Bohemian Rhapsody, The Favourite, Green Book, Roma, A Star is Born, and Vice for Best Picture at the Oscars next month.
